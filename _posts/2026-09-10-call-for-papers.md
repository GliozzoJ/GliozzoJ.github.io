I am pleased to invite submissions to the *Frontiers in Medicine* Research Topic **"Multi-Omics Integration for Clinical Decision-Making: Methods, Models, and Applications"**, for which I am serving as Guest Editor. 📄 💻 🧬

This Research Topic aims to advance the use of multi-omics integration for clinical decision-making by bringing together methodological innovations, computational frameworks, and translational applications with demonstrated clinical relevance.

Feel free to contact us for questions and share the call! 📨 

Submit your article by 30 November 2026.
