Experience

  1. Jan 2026 – Present

    Bioinformatician

    Translational Immunology Unit, Fondazione IRCCS Istituto Nazionale dei Tumori · Milan, Italy

    • Analysis of multi-omics longitudinal data from blood samples of breast cancer patients to evaluate the effect of a fast-mimicking diet on immunity.
    • Support wet-lab team members in bioinformatics analysis (mostly RNA-sequencing downstreaming analysis), including experimental design, data interpretation, and hypothesis generation.
    • Development of an R package for the analysis for longitudinal data.
    • Ongoing collaboration with the BiomiX consortium to evaluate the updated multi-omics integration pipelines and write a research work.
    • Ongoing collaboration with University of Milan to evaluate the problem of selection bias in GCN-based multi-omics integration pipelines.

    Initial engagement began through short-term and collaborative roles within the same unit.

  2. Feb 2024 – Aug 2025

    Postdoctoral Research Fellow

    Department of Computer Science, Università degli Studi di Milano · Milan, Italy

    Developed and applied patient-similarity-network and graph-based methods for multi-omics integration. Activities included the validation and publication of miss-SNF; acute myeloid leukaemia patient-subtyping analyses; BiomiX pipeline development; pancreatic-cancer multi-omics analyses; and co-supervision of two MSc theses on multimodal integration and graph neural networks.

  3. Nov 2020 – Jan 2024

    PhD Student

    Università degli Studi di Milano & Joint Research Centre, European Commission · Italy

    PhD within the framework of the Collaborative Doctoral Partnership programme between the University of Milan and the Joint Research Centre (JRC), Directorate-General of the European Commission (Ispra, VA). Activities include:

    • Literature review on multimodal integration approaches for the construction of patient similarity networks.
    • Development of a multimodal data-fusion pipeline leveraging block-wise analysis to adaptively reduce data dimensionality through the estimation of intrinsic dimensionality.
    • Development of miss-SNF, a method for the integration of multimodal data in the presence of patients with completely missing modalities.

  4. Feb 2020 – Oct 2020

    Researcher

    Neuroradiology Unit, IRCCS Ospedale San Raffaele · Milan, Italy

    Developed a pipeline for myelin-water-fraction map computation in paediatric and metachromatic-leukodystrophy cohorts from brain MRI data, including U-Net-based brain segmentation, co-registration to the Myelin Water Atlas, and regional statistical analysis.

  5. Mar 2017 – Jan 2020

    Bioinformatician

    Dermatology Unit, Fondazione IRCCS Ca' Granda – Ospedale Maggiore Policlinico · Milan, Italy

    DNA- and RNA-sequencing analysis to identify diagnostic, prognostic, and therapeutic biomarkers in blastic plasmacytoid dendritic cell neoplasm and aggressive epidermotropic primary cutaneous CD8 T-cell lymphoma.

  6. Oct 2018 & Nov 2019

    Visiting Researcher

    Kircher Lab, Computational Genome Biology Group, Berlin Institute of Health & Charité · Berlin, Germany

    Contributed to a MIUR–DAAD Joint Mobility Program project developing machine-learning methods to prioritise regulatory variants in human disease.

  7. Mar 2016 – Jun 2016

    Visiting Researcher

    PaccanaroLab, Centre for Systems Biology, Royal Holloway, University of London · Egham, United Kingdom

    Conducted an international research collaboration as part of the Master's thesis, supported by a University of Milan scholarship.
